Core capabilities

Everything required for an explainable academic pathway

The prototype combines graph intelligence, deterministic validation, and human governance.

01

Knowledge graph

Connects courses, prerequisite knowledge, credits, departments, electives, and curriculum relationships.

02

Graph-RAG retrieval

Retrieves relevant course and policy evidence with source status and traceable citations.

03

Personalized pathways

Uses the student’s completed subjects and current semester to build a candidate semester-wise plan.

04

Conflict detection

Finds completed-course conflicts, readiness gaps, unknown course codes, and sourced formal constraints.

05

Risk and bottlenecks

Highlights high-impact remaining courses and explains the factors affecting planning risk.

06

Career alignment

Matches remaining curriculum choices to AI, backend, cybersecurity, and data career skills.

07

Course substitutions

Suggests candidate alternatives while separating recommendations from approved equivalencies.

08

Faculty governance

Creates an evidence packet for waiver and substitution cases without automatically approving exceptions.

Agent workflow

From a student question to verified advice

Every response follows a visible, source-aware reasoning pipeline.

  1. 01

    Understand the student

    The Profile Agent reads subjects, grades, 0–10 GPA values, earned credits, career goals, and current semester.

    PROFILE
  2. 02

    Route the request

    The Supervisor identifies whether the request concerns prerequisites, pathways, risks, careers, substitutions, policies, or waivers.

    ROUTE
  3. 03

    Retrieve graph evidence

    Graph-RAG combines curriculum relationships with relevant course descriptions and policy sections.

    RETRIEVE
  4. 04

    Run the specialist agent

    The selected deterministic agent calculates conflicts, pathways, bottlenecks, career alignment, or substitution candidates.

    ANALYZE
  5. 05

    Verify formal constraints

    The verification layer prevents advisory prerequisite knowledge from being presented as an official registration block.

    VERIFY
  6. 06

    Explain, cite, or escalate

    The advisor returns traceable advice, while exceptional waiver or substitution cases are prepared for faculty review.

    RESPOND
